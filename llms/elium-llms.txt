# Elium

> Elium is a knowledge management SaaS app that helps teams capture, structure, and share company knowledge.

Elium is a European enterprise knowledge platform, used by 150,000+ users across 150+ organisations. It serves governed knowledge to people and to AI agents: teams capture and validate knowledge once, then deliver it through AI search, assistants, portals and integrations. Pages below are in English; French versions live under /fr (for example https://elium.com/fr/pricing). The complete URL list is in https://elium.com/sitemap.xml.

Blog posts and customer stories are also served as markdown: append .md to the page URL. Product documentation lives at https://help.elium.com.

## Platform and product

- [Platform](https://elium.com/platform): One platform setup shared by every team: AI, sovereignty, connectors and identity.
- [AI Search](https://elium.com/product/ai-search): One AI search across the knowledge base, for people and AI agents, with cited and permission-aware answers.
- [Smart Assistants](https://elium.com/product/smart-assistants): Conversational AI assistants grounded in the knowledge base, scoped to a team or topic.
- [Connect with your AI](https://elium.com/product/connect-your-ai): Serve governed knowledge to your own AI tools through MCP, an open API and native connectors.
- [AI Import](https://elium.com/product/ai-import): Import files, drives or whole platforms; AI turns them into structured native content.
- [Content Editor](https://elium.com/product/content-editor): Structured block-based editor with AI drafting, inline translation and rich media.
- [Structured Templates](https://elium.com/product/templates): Templates with predefined fields that keep every article consistent.
- [Decision Tree](https://elium.com/product/decision-tree): Guided step-by-step trees for support ticket resolution.
- [Multilingual Knowledge](https://elium.com/product/multilingual): One governed article per topic, translated in place; readers and AI get answers in their own language.
- [Multi-Step Approval](https://elium.com/product/multi-step-approval): Configurable sign-off flows before content goes live.
- [Expiration Management](https://elium.com/product/expiration-management): Verification periods and alerts that keep knowledge current.
- [Duplicate Detection](https://elium.com/product/duplicate-detection): AI flags semantic overlaps before publication and suggests merges.
- [Gap Detection](https://elium.com/product/gap-detection): Finds the questions your knowledge cannot answer and routes them to an owner.
- [Digest & Alerts](https://elium.com/product/digest-alerts): Proactive notifications and personalised digests.
- [360° Analytics](https://elium.com/product/analytics): One analytics layer across content, search and AI usage.
- [External Portals](https://elium.com/product/external-portals): Self-service knowledge portals for employees, customers and partners.
